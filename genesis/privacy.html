Privacy notice

GENESIS: Hold the Station · last updated 26 October 2026

Who runs GENESIS

GENESIS is made by TLG Marketing (TLG Marketing Games on Google Play). Contact: armin@tlgmarketing.com.

What stays on your device

Your species (progress, settings, records) is saved on this device (in the app's storage, or in your browser's storage when you play on the web), and a copy is kept online as described under "Cloud save". The game also remembers, on this device only, whether you answered the statistics question, which news you have read and when this device last synced with the cloud.

Cloud save

To keep your save safe and to prevent cheating, the game stores your game progress and an anonymous account ID on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, in the European Union). The game creates the anonymous account by itself: there is no sign-up, and no name, email address or other personal data is involved. What is stored is your save (progress, settings and records), a short summary of it (best wave, runs, currencies and upgrade levels), the game version and the time of each upload. The online copy is the one the game uses; the server only accepts progress that could have been earned in the time since the last upload. Uploads happen every few minutes while you play and when you leave the game, and only while you are online. This applies to the web version and to the Android and iOS apps. Google processes this data for us as a service provider. To have your cloud save deleted, write to armin@tlgmarketing.com with the account ID shown under Settings → Cloud save.

Accounts, tournaments and colonies

If you create a tournament account, the server stores your player name, a salted hash of your password, a hash of your recovery code, your tournament entries and results, your colony membership and the colony chat messages you send. Cloud save stores one copy of your exported save for your account.

Anonymous usage statistics (only if you agree)

If you choose "Share statistics", the game sends events such as "a life started", "a life ended at wave 34 in the Primordial Pool", "research started" and "a section was opened", with a random install number created on your device. No name, account, email or save is attached. Saying no, or switching the setting off later, stops sending and deletes the install number from your device. Statistics are kept for 90 days.

Crash reports

When the game hits an error, it sends the error message, the location in the game's code, the game version and your browser's user-agent string, so the problem can be fixed. Crash reports contain no account, save or install number.

Feedback

Feedback you send contains your message, an optional rating, the game version and how many lives your species has played. Do not include personal details you do not want to share.

Chat reports and moderation

When a player reports a colony chat message, a copy of the message, its author's player name and the reason are kept for review. Three reports hide a message until it is reviewed. If a report is upheld, the author's chat can be paused for seven days.

The Android and iOS apps

The GENESIS apps from Google Play and the App Store keep your species in the app's storage on your phone and back it up with the cloud save described above. They have no tournament account, colonies, statistics, crash reports, feedback form, ads or purchases, and they send nothing to the GENESIS server. When more online features, ads or purchases come to the apps, this notice will say what they send before the update is released.

Advertising and purchases

This build shows placeholder ads and has no payment provider, so no ad network or payment service receives data from it. When an ad network or payment provider is connected, it will process data under its own privacy policy, which will be linked here, and personalised ads will only be shown with your consent.

Your choices and rights

  • Switch statistics on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Export or delete your local save from your browser at any time.
  • Ask the operator for a copy of, or deletion of, the data stored with your account or your cloud save: armin@tlgmarketing.com.

Children

GENESIS is not directed at children under 13 (16 in some countries), and the apps collect no personal data from anyone: the cloud save holds only game progress and an anonymous account ID.
